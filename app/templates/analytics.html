Key Performance Metrics

Key Metrics Explained:
• Total Consumed: All electricity you've used from both solar and grid
• Total Produced: All electricity your solar panels have generated
• Net Energy: Negative = you produced more than used (good!), Positive = you used more than produced
• Self-Consumption: Percentage of your electricity that came from your own solar panels (higher is better)

Daily Energy Flow
Daily Energy Flow Chart: This chart shows your daily electricity consumption (red line) and solar production (green line) over time. The blue dashed line shows your net energy balance - when it's below zero, you produced more than you consumed that day (good!). When it's above zero, you consumed more than you produced.
Monthly Trends
Monthly Trends: This bar chart compares your total monthly electricity consumption (red bars) vs solar production (green bars). Taller green bars mean better solar production months. Compare the heights to see seasonal patterns - typically summer months have higher solar production.
Net Energy Balance
Net Energy Balance: Green bars (negative values) mean you were a net producer that month - you generated more electricity than you used! Red bars (positive values) mean you consumed more than you produced. The goal is to have as many green bars as possible.
Hourly Average Pattern
Hourly Pattern: Shows your average electricity consumption for each hour of the day. Peak hours (usually evening) will show higher bars. This helps you understand when you use most electricity - useful for optimizing usage during cheaper off-peak hours.
Daily Average Pattern
Daily Pattern: Compares your average consumption across different days of the week. You might notice weekends have different usage patterns than weekdays due to being home more or different activities.
Monthly Average Pattern
Monthly Pattern: Shows seasonal consumption patterns. Winter months typically show higher consumption (heating), while summer might be lower. This helps predict future consumption and identify seasonal trends.
Time-of-Use Analysis
Time-of-Use Analysis: This pie chart breaks down your consumption by time periods. "Peak" hours (7 AM - 9 PM) usually cost more than "Off-Peak" hours. The goal is to shift usage to off-peak periods when possible to save money on your electricity bill.
Energy Independence Trend
Energy Independence: Shows what percentage of your daily electricity needs were met by your solar panels. 100% means you were completely self-sufficient that day. Higher percentages are better - it means you relied less on the electrical grid.
Grid Export Percentage
Grid Export: Shows what percentage of your solar production was exported back to the electrical grid (not used immediately). Lower percentages are generally better - it means you're using more of your own solar energy directly, which saves more money than selling it back.
Efficiency Metrics Summary
Efficiency Metrics: These cards show your solar system's performance. "Avg Energy Independence" is how much of your electricity comes from solar. "Self-Sufficient Days" counts days when you produced more than you used. Higher numbers indicate better solar system performance and more savings.
HEP Tariff Configuration
Adjust settings to match your HEP electricity contract
Netting Ratio: HEP typically credits 70-80% of retail price for excess solar energy returned to grid
Cost vs Income
Cost vs Income: This pie chart shows how much you paid for electricity from the grid (red) versus how much money you earned by selling excess solar power back to the grid (green). The larger the green portion, the more money you earned from your solar panels.
Solar Savings Breakdown
Savings Breakdown: Shows different ways your solar panels save you money. "Self-Consumption Savings" is money saved by using your own solar power instead of buying from the grid. "Feed-in Income" is money earned by selling excess power. All amounts are in Euros (EUR).
Financial Summary
Financial Summary: "Net Cost" is your actual electricity bill after solar savings. "Total Savings" shows how much money you saved compared to having no solar panels. "Savings %" shows what percentage of your electricity costs were eliminated by solar. Monthly/Yearly projections estimate future savings based on current patterns.
